Suburbiæ Unexpected Fest

Co-Founder · Creative Director · Advertising Manager · Social Media Manager · Graphic Designer · Media Strategist · Content Creator

Suburbiæ Unexpected Fest is an independent music and arts festival based in Teramo, Abruzzo. Born from the cultural association JAY27, the festival brings together alternative music, visual culture, exhibitions, food and underground communities in a natural open-air setting. As Co-Founder and Creative Director, I contribute to the festival’s concept, visual identity, communication strategy, advertising campaigns, social media direction, graphic design, content production and overall creative development.
